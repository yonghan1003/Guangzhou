Day 1

Arrival & Old Guangzhou

Settle in, explore the historic heart, and taste your first Cantonese feast
Morning · Arrival
✈ Flight CZ8490 → Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN)
Land at Baiyun Airport. Take the Airport Express or Metro Line 3 to your hotel. Check in and freshen up. Stay suggestion: Book near Beijing Road or Zhujiang New Town for easy metro access.
🚇 Metro ~¥8 ✈ CZ8490 Arrival
Lunch · 12:00 PM
🥟 Dim Sum at Tao Tao Ju (陶陶居)
Start your trip the Cantonese way — with yum cha! This legendary teahouse has been serving dim sum since 1880. Must-order: har gow (shrimp dumplings), char siu bao (BBQ pork buns), and cheung fun (rice noodle rolls).
🍽 Cantonese Dim Sum 💰 ~¥50-80/person
Afternoon · 2:00 PM
🏛 Chen Clan Ancestral Hall (陈家祠)
A masterpiece of Lingnan architecture! This Qing Dynasty clan hall features incredible wood, stone, and brick carvings. The complex now houses the Guangdong Folk Art Museum — allow 1.5–2 hours to explore.
📸 Stunning architecture 💰 ¥10 entry
Late Afternoon · 4:30 PM
🛍 Beijing Road Pedestrian Street (北京路)
Stroll down this 2,000-year-old shopping street! See the preserved ancient road layers under glass panels, browse shops, and grab street snacks along the way.
🛍 Shopping & History
Evening · 7:00 PM
🌃 Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street (上下九)
Neon-lit Qilou (arcade buildings) line this bustling street — a perfect evening stroll. Sample local snacks: double-skin milk pudding, shrimp rice rolls, and stinky tofu. End with a bowl of Guangzhou fish noodles.
🍢 Street Food Heaven 📸 Qilou Architecture
💡 Day 1 Tip — Budget Saver

Set up Alipay metro QR for tap-and-go — no card deposit needed! Or get a Guangzhou Tong Card (羊城通) at any metro station. Also: many of today's spots are FREE — Beijing Road, Shangxiajiu, and the street food scene cost very little!

Day 2

Yuexiu, Baiyun & Skyline Views

Park exploration, iconic landmarks, and Guangzhou's modern skyline at sunset
Morning · 8:30 AM
🌳 Yuexiu Park & Five Rams Sculpture (越秀公园)
Guangzhou's largest urban park and home to the iconic Five Rams statue — the city's symbol! Also visit the Zhenhai Tower (Five-Story Tower) which houses a history museum. A peaceful morning walk among banyan trees.
📸 Five Rams Icon 💰 Free
Mid-Morning · 10:30 AM
🏛 Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall (中山纪念堂)
A stunning octagonal hall surrounded by gardens. The architecture blends traditional Chinese and Western styles. It's just a short walk from Yuexiu Park.
💰 ¥10 entry
Lunch · 12:30 PM
🍜 Wonton Noodles at Ngau Kee Beef (牛记)
Try classic Guangzhou wonton noodles (云吞面) — silky shrimp wontons in rich broth with springy egg noodles. A staple of Cantonese comfort food. Pair with braised beef brisket!
🍜 Cantonese Noodles 💰 ~¥30-50
Afternoon · 2:00 PM
🏙 Zhujiang New Town (珠江新城)
Guangzhou's modern CBD! Walk along the Zhujiang River, admire the IFC Tower and Guangzhou Opera House (Zaha Hadid design). Visit the Guangdong Museum (free, just need ID) for a deep dive into Lingnan culture.
📸 Modern Skyline 🎭 Opera House
Evening · 6:30 PM
🗼 Canton Tower (广州塔) — The Pearl River Night View
The crown jewel of any Guangzhou trip! Head up to the observation deck (488m!) for breathtaking 360° views. Book tickets online in advance to skip the queue. Stay for the stunning night views — the tower itself lights up in rainbow colors!
📸 Must-Visit! 💰 ¥150 or FREE ground views 🚇 Metro to Canton Tower Station
💡 Day 2 Tip — Save Money!

Canton Tower is pricey (¥150). Budget option: Skip the observation deck and enjoy the stunning views from the ground for free — the tower lights up beautifully at night! Or visit the free outdoor observation area at Zhujiang New Town instead. If you do go up, buy tickets on Trip.com at least 1 day in advance for discounts.

Day 3

Shamian Island & Cultural Gems

Colonial charm, river breezes, and hidden cultural treasures
Morning · 9:00 AM
🏝 Shamian Island (沙面岛)
A magical pedestrian island that feels like a European village. Lined with 19th-century colonial buildings, banyan-shaded walkways, and charming cafes. Perfect for a slow morning stroll and photography. Don't miss the former British and French Concession buildings!
📸 Photography Paradise 💰 Free
Lunch · 12:00 PM
🐟 Seafood Lunch at Liwan Lake Park Area
Head to the Liwan district for authentic Cantonese seafood. Try steamed fish with ginger and scallion, salt-and-pepper prawns, and stir-fried water spinach with garlic. This is where locals eat!
🐟 Cantonese Seafood 💰 ~¥60-100/person
Afternoon · 2:00 PM
🌺 Liwan Lake Park (荔湾湖公园)
A serene traditional Chinese garden park with lotus ponds, willow trees, and covered bridges. If you're lucky, you'll catch local musicians practicing Cantonese opera in the pavilions!
🌿 Traditional Garden 💰 Free
Late Afternoon · 4:00 PM
🎨 Xiguan Folk Art & Mansion Tour
Explore the historic Xiguan (西关) neighborhood — the heart of old Guangzhou. Visit the Lingnan Impression shops, watch traditional candy-pulling art, and see exquisite Xiguan-style tiled houses. If time allows, visit the Shijing Xuantai Museum hidden underground.
🎭 Cultural Experience 📸 Tiled Houses
Evening · 7:00 PM
🚢 Pearl River Night Cruise (珠江夜游)
The ultimate Guangzhou evening! Board a cruise from Tianzi Wharf and glide past the illuminated Canton Tower, Liede Bridge, and the stunning CBD skyline reflected in the river. Take a seat on the open deck and soak it all in.
📸 Unmissable Views 💰 ~¥60-80 (discounted) 🚇 Metro to Tianzi Wharf Station
💡 Day 3 Tip — Save on Cruise!

Budget alternative: Skip the cruise and walk along the Zhujiang River Promenade for FREE — you'll still get stunning skyline views and can see the Canton Tower light up! If you do cruise, the evening cruise (7:00–9:00 PM) is best. Book via Trip.com for discounted tickets (~¥60-80).

Day 4

Day Trip: Foshan or Baiyun Mountain

Choose your adventure — martial arts heritage or mountain hiking
Option A · Full Day
🥋 Day Trip to Foshan (佛山) — Martial Arts Capital
Just 30 minutes by metro! Visit the Foshan Ancestral Temple (祖庙), the Ip Man Museum, and Bruce Lee's birthplace. Watch a martial arts performance at the temple grounds. Stroll through Donghua Ancient Town (东华里) for beautiful Lingnan architecture.
🚇 Metro Line 1 to Foshan 📸 Martial Arts Heritage 💰 Temple ¥25, mostly free
Option A · Lunch
🍳 Foshan's Famous Double-Skin Milk (双皮奶)
Foshan is famous for its silky double-skin milk pudding — a silky, sweet custard dessert. Try it at Renxin Double-Skin Milk (仁信老铺). Also sample Foshan's special rice dumplings (裹蒸粽).
🍮 Famous Dessert
Option B · Full Day
⛰ Baiyun Mountain (白云山)
Guangzhou's beloved "back garden" — a lush mountain park right in the city. Take the cable car up, hike the scenic trails, and enjoy panoramic views of the entire city. Visit Mingchun Garden and the Star Lake along the way.
📸 Panoramic Views 🚇 Metro to Baiyun Mountain 💰 ¥5 entry + ¥25 cable car
Option B · Lunch
🍲 Mountain Picnic or Yunbing Restaurant
Pack a light picnic or eat at one of the mountain's teahouse restaurants with city views. In the evening, head to Tianhe City (天河城) for shopping, dinner, and the vibrant nightlife scene.
🍲 Mountain Dining
Evening · 7:30 PM
🍜 Dinner at Zhujiang Beer Street (珠江琶醍)
End the day at Guangzhou's riverside bar and dining strip. Craft beers, live music, and a stunning river view. Perfect for a relaxed evening after a full day of exploration.
🍺 Riverside Dining 🎵 Live Music
💡 Day 4 Tip — Budget Choice

Both options are metro-accessible (no taxis needed). Baiyun Mountain is the cheaper choice (~¥30 entry + cable car), while Foshan is slightly more (¥25 temple + food). Skip the cable car and hike the mountain trails to save even more — the views are just as rewarding!

Day 5

Temple, Markets & Departure

Final morning explorations, souvenir shopping, and a fond farewell
Morning · 8:00 AM
🪷 Six Banyan Trees Temple (六榕寺)
One of Guangzhou's oldest Buddhist temples (dating to 537 AD). The beautiful flower pagoda is a must-see. A peaceful way to start your final day — take a moment to reflect on your trip.
📸 Ancient Pagoda 💰 Free (donations welcome)
Mid-Morning · 10:00 AM
☕ Morning Tea at Jianshe Liu Ma Lu (建设六马路)
Guangzhou's "cafe street" with a European vibe. Enjoy a final cup of tea or coffee at a charming cafe, or do one last round of dim sum — this time at a quieter neighborhood teahouse.
☕ Cafe Culture
Late Morning · 11:30 AM
🛍 Souvenir Shopping at Wuxiang Market or Liwan Plaza
Pick up last-minute souvenirs: Cantonese tea sets, dried seafood, herbal tea packs, chopsticks, and silk products. Bargain-friendly markets! Don't forget to grab some egg tarts and almond cookies for the road.
🛍 Souvenirs 🍪 Snacks to Take Home
Lunch · 1:00 PM
🍚 Final Cantonese Feast
One last memorable meal! Try roasted goose rice (烧鹅饭) or a Cantonese claypot rice (煲仔饭) at a local restaurant. Savor every bite — Guangzhou food will be the thing you miss most!
🍚 Cantonese Farewell
Afternoon · 3:00 PM
✈ Flight CZ8301 — Head to Airport & Departure
Take the Airport Express or Metro Line 3 back to Baiyun International Airport. Allow 2–3 hours before your flight for check-in and security. Happy travels! 祝你旅途愉快!
🚇 Metro Line 3 to Airport 💰 ~¥8 ✈ CZ8301 Departure
💡 Day 5 Tip — Budget Saver

Most of today is completely free — the Six Banyan Trees Temple, cafe strolling, and the Flower Market all cost nothing! Take a last free walk through Flower Market (花城广场) near the CBD for a beautiful farewell photo before heading to the airport.

🍜 Guangzhou Food Guide

Guangzhou is the culinary capital of China. Here are the must-try dishes during your visit.

💡 Budget Tip: Eat at local canteens (食堂) for ¥15–25 per meal, or grab street snacks for ¥10–20. Skip tourist restaurants — follow the locals to find the best AND cheapest food!
🥟

Dim Sum

早茶 · zǎo chá

The heart of Cantonese breakfast culture. Order from carts or menus at teahouses — har gow, siu mai, char siu bao, cheung fun, and more.

🏆 Best at: Tao Tao Ju, Panxi, Dim Dim Sum
🍗

Roast Goose & BBQ Meats

烧鹅 · shāo é

Cantonese roast meats are legendary. Crispy-skinned roast goose, char siu (BBQ pork), and roast duck — all carved to order over rice.

🏆 Best at: Yat Lok, Lou Heung
🍲

Claypot Rice

煲仔饭 · bāo zǎi fàn

Rice cooked in a clay pot with Chinese sausage, chicken, or minced pork. The crispy rice crust at the bottom is the best part!

🏆 Best at: Jiaji Claypot Rice
🥣

Cantonese Soup

老火汤 · lǎo huǒ tāng

Slow-cooked for hours with herbs, meat, and vegetables. Every restaurant has its daily soup — it's a Cantonese health ritual!

🏆 Best at: Any local Cantonese restaurant
🍮

Double-Skin Milk Pudding

双皮奶 · shuāng pí nǎi

A silky, warm or cold milk pudding with a delicate skin on top. Sweet, creamy, and absolutely addictive. A Foshan specialty!

🏆 Best at: Renxin, Nanshun Shuangpi Nai
🐟

Steamed Fish

清蒸鱼 · qīng zhēng yú

Fresh whole fish steamed with ginger, scallions, and soy sauce. Simple, pure, and the hallmark of Cantonese cooking philosophy.

🏆 Best at: Any seafood restaurant in Liwan

📋 Practical Tips

Everything you need to know before arriving in Guangzhou.

💳 Money & Payment

  • China is almost entirely cashless — WeChat Pay and Alipay dominate
  • Foreign visitors can now link international credit cards to WeChat/Alipay
  • Keep some cash (¥200-500) for small vendors and emergencies
  • ATMs at airports and banks (ICBC, Bank of China) accept foreign cards
  • Exchange rates: ~¥7.2 = $1 USD (check before travel)

🚇 Transportation (Budget Mode)

  • Metro only — clean, fast, and cheap (¥2-8 per ride). Avoid taxis/DiDi to save money!
  • Download Alipay for QR code metro entry (no physical card needed!)
  • Use Amap (高德地图) for best metro route planning
  • Airport Express to city center: ~30 min, ¥25 (cheapest option to airport)
  • Walk whenever possible — Guangzhou is very walkable, especially in old town areas!

📱 Apps to Download

  • WeChat — messaging, payments, mini-programs (essential!)
  • Alipay — payments, metro QR, food ordering
  • DiDi — ride-hailing
  • Trip.com — English booking for hotels & attractions
  • Google Translate — camera translation for menus
  • Amap (高德地图) — best navigation for China

🌡 November Weather

  • Temperature: 18°C – 26°C (64°F – 79°F)
  • Humidity: Moderate, much lower than summer
  • Rain: Low chance — pack a light rain jacket just in case
  • Clothing: Light layers — t-shirts, light jacket, comfortable walking shoes
  • November is one of the best months to visit!

🏨 Where to Stay (Budget-Friendly)

  • Beijing Road area: Central, walkable, tons of cheap eats nearby
  • Liwan District: Traditional, authentic, most affordable hotels
  • Budget guesthouses & hostels: ¥80–150/night (private room available!)
  • Book on Trip.com, Booking.com, or Hostelworld for deals
  • Tip: staying near a metro station saves transport costs too!

🛂 Visa & Entry

  • Many nationalities qualify for the 144-hour visa-free transit policy
  • Check if your country qualifies (63 countries listed)
  • Transit must be to a third country (not round-trip to origin)
  • Passport must be valid for 6+ months
  • Consider the standard tourist visa (L visa) for 5+ day stays
